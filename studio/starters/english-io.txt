# Outline

You may take up to 10 bullet points into the oral. Keep each one short: these are prompts, not a script.

1. Introduce the global issue and why it matters.
2. Introduce the extract from the first work and its place in the whole work.
3. First point: how the author presents the global issue in the extract.
4. Evidence and analysis from the extract.
5. Link to the rest of the first work.
6. Introduce the extract from the second work.
7. How the second work presents the global issue.
8. Evidence and analysis from the second extract.
9. Compare the two works' choices and their effects.
10. Conclude: what the two works show about the global issue.
